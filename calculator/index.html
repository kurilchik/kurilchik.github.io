Privacy Policy for Calculator

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Calculator (package name com.kurylchyk.calculator) handles information when you use the app.

Calculator does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other contact information. Calculations are performed on your device and are not sent to the developer.

Advertising and information processed by Google

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as:

  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system, language, and app interactions;
  • advertising impressions, clicks, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals.

This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and secure advertising, depending on your consent choices and applicable law. Google processes this information under its own terms and policies. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, a Google-certified consent message may ask for your choices before personalized advertising is shown. Depending on your choice and applicable law, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all advertising.

Calculator data

Numbers, operations, and results entered into Calculator are used only to perform calculations on your device. The developer does not receive or retain this information on a server.

Legal bases and international transfers

Where applicable, advertising-related processing may rely on your consent, legitimate interests, or another legal basis described in the consent message and Google's policies. Google and its partners may process information in countries outside your country of residence, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Data retention

The developer does not operate a server that stores your calculations or advertising data. Google and its advertising partners determine their own retention periods as described in their policies.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. Requests concerning data processed directly by Google should also be directed to Google through its privacy tools.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to limit the app's data handling, but no electronic processing or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
